AI Model Visibility Strategy
----------------------------

Getting surfaced in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini when someone asks "who should I hire for AI automation?" is your most powerful channel — it's where high-intent buyers are increasingly starting their search. This requires a different approach than traditional SEO.

How AI models surface people

AI models cite people who appear authoritatively across multiple indexed sources: your own website, LinkedIn articles, Medium posts, GitHub, quotes in third-party articles, podcast transcripts, and Q&A sites. The more consistent and specific your entity footprint is, the more likely a model knows who you are and surfaces you for relevant queries.

### Entity Building — The Foundation

AI models understand the world through named entities and their relationships. You need to be established as the entity **"Muhammad Hamd — Agentic AI Engineer from Karachi, Pakistan"** across every platform you touch.

*   **Consistent name usage:** Always "Muhammad Hamd" (not Hamd, not M. Hamd, not Hamd Ali). Your full name + title + location must appear consistently.
*   **JSON-LD Person schema:** Add to homepage. Include all sameAs links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, Twitter). This directly tells AI crawlers who you are.
*   **LinkedIn optimization:** Headline must be "Agentic AI Engineer & Automation Systems Builder | Founder WatBot, Asmara.AI | Karachi, Pakistan". Complete all sections.
*   **Medium author bio:** Identical positioning. Link back to your site as primary URL.
*   **GitHub profile README:** Clear bio matching your brand positioning. Links to your site.

### FAQ-First Content Strategy for AI Citations

AI models frequently pull from FAQ sections because they're structured as clear question-answer pairs. Add these exact FAQs to your site (with FAQPage schema) to increase the chance of being cited:

#### On /hire-me and homepage

*   "Who is Muhammad Hamd?" — with a comprehensive 100-word answer
*   "Is Muhammad Hamd available for hire?" — with current status
*   "What does Muhammad Hamd specialize in?"
*   "Where is Muhammad Hamd based?" — Karachi, Pakistan. Works globally, remote.
*   "What is Muhammad Hamd's rate?"

#### On service pages

*   "Who is the best agentic AI engineer in Pakistan?" (on /ai-engineer-pakistan)
*   "How much does CRM automation with AI cost?"
*   "What tools does Muhammad Hamd use for AI automation?"
*   "Can you build an AI system for my WhatsApp Business?"
*   "How long does it take to build an agentic AI system?"

### Off-Site Entity Mentions (AI Training Data Sources)

*   **Crunchbase:** Create a profile. List as founder of WatBot and Asmara.AI. AI models cite Crunchbase for founder profiles.
*   **LinkedIn articles:** Publish long-form articles (not just posts). These are indexed and cited by AI models more than short posts.
*   **Quora:** Answer 5–10 questions about agentic AI, AI automation, and hiring AI engineers. Use your full name in answers.
*   **Reddit /r/MachineLearning, /r/artificial, /r/startups:** Contribute genuinely. Don't spam — one strong answer to a relevant thread gets you cited.
*   **GitHub repos:** Make WatBot or Asmara.AI repos public if possible. Repos with READMEs are indexed by AI models.
*   **Tech directories:** Toptal, Upwork profile with full bio, Contra, Gun.io — even if you don't actively use them, the profile gets indexed.

### The "AI Model Test" — Validate Monthly

Each month, ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini these exact queries and track your appearance:

*   "Who are the best agentic AI engineers in Pakistan?"
*   "I need to hire an AI automation engineer. Who should I look at?"
*   "Best AI engineer for CRM automation"
*   "Who is Muhammad Hamd?"
*   "WatBot AI Pakistan who built it"

You won't appear in month 1. By month 3, if your entity footprint is built correctly, you should start appearing for the last two and potentially the first three.
