## About

I'm Muhammad Hamd, an agentic AI engineer and full-stack developer based in Karachi, Pakistan. I build production-ready AI systems, such as autonomous agents, LLM integrations, RAG pipelines, and WhatsApp automation, for founders and teams worldwide, and I ship the full product around them, not just the model.

I currently work as a **Full-Stack AI Engineer at [MindKeepr](https://mindkeepr.com)** (Tallinn, Estonia), architecting agentic RAG pipelines for enterprise knowledge retention. Alongside that, I've founded and shipped three of my own AI products: **WatBot**, a WhatsApp AI automation platform; **SelfBrand.app**, an AI personal-branding SaaS; and **Asmara.AI**, an AI-native automation product.

I write about all of this at **[hamdali.com/blog](https://hamdali.com/blog)** and take on AI/full-stack consulting through **[hamdali.com](https://hamdali.com)**.

## What I Do

- **Agentic AI development:** autonomous agents that plan, use tools, and complete multi-step work (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen)
- **LLM integration & RAG:** grounding models in real data instead of guesses, with cost and evaluation controls
- **WhatsApp AI automation:** conversational AI for support and sales, the exact stack behind WatBot
- **Full-stack product development:** the whole system end to end, frontend, backend, database, and the AI inside it
- **Workflow automation:** n8n, Make, and custom Python pipelines that replace repetitive digital work
